Who We Are

The Trusted Bridge Between Global Ambition and Indonesian Opportunity

The Gateway is a specialized advisory firm that helps international investors, shipping lines, logistics operators, and industrial players successfully enter, expand, and optimize their presence in Indonesia.

Indonesia’s archipelago geography, rapid infrastructure development, and complex regulatory environment create both significant opportunity and operational complexity. We turn that complexity into clarity — combining deep local knowledge with global best practices in ports, logistics, and transportation.
