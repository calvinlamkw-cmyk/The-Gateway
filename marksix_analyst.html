Prize-Relevant Number Patterns (Historical 49-Ball Draws)

Focused on patterns that actually appeared in past draws. A pattern of size k is a set of k main numbers that sat inside a draw’s 6 main balls. The more draws contain a pattern, the more often a ticket holding that pattern could have secured at least the corresponding HKJC prize tier (see mapping below).
